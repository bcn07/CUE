## What was built today

- **Sheet sign-ups enrol themselves** (`server/signup_sync.py`): the director polls the desk pages' Apps Script and turns each consenting row into a person with both Drive photos and the dashboard's camera. Endpoint comes from `server/static/config.js` (already set up) or `CUE_SIGNUP_ENDPOINT`. Never restarts the Deepgram stream. 25 tests, plus a review with 8 confirmed findings fixed. Not yet tried with a real phone sign-up.
- **Deepgram Flux** (`CUE_DEEPGRAM_MODEL=flux-general-en`, set in `.env`): turn-based model on the v2 endpoint, one clause per sentence per turn. Measured on the real API: final clause 0.85 to 1.0 s after audio ends (nova-3: 0.55 s, but nova-3 splits mid-sentence). Key terms update in place, no reconnect on roster change.
- **Mic selected by name** in `.env` (`CUE_MIC_DEVICE="MacBook Pro Microphone"`): a USB audio device had taken slot 1 and the director was transcribing silence.
- **Desk: a tile for every camera.** Per role the live camera takes the tile; extra cameras get their own tile (Guest 2, key 4). Verified in headless Chrome.
- **Setup page's director link opens /desk**; classic view kept as a secondary link.
- **"Let's look at the TV / screen / whiteboard / audience"** is understood by the rules, not only the LLM. Cuts to the camera whose role is `demo` (or `audience`), otherwise wide. Never a guest.
- **Scene tags** (`server/scene.py`, OFF by default, `CUE_SCENE=1`): a local vision model tags what each camera sees, and "let's look at the flowers" cuts to the one camera whose tags show flowers, wide if two do. Off because it keeps a second 3B model resident and Ollama died once today with three models loaded, which also took the cue interpreter down. Ollama is now running as `brew services start ollama`.
- **LAN HTTPS server for sign-ups** (`tools/serve_https.py`, from upstream PR #5).
